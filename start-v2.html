In short

Who is it for?

Teachers, school leaders and parents, and anyone who wants to teach something to a class.

Do I need an account?

No, you can look around freely. You only need an account to take part.

What happens to my data?

It is kept in Switzerland and the EU. You can download it or delete your account at any time.

Everything done here is noted in a log that cannot be changed afterwards. That way a parent, a school or an auditor can check for themselves what happened, and when.
