# Atrium

The Atrium is the coming forum of the entrosana Hub: the place to talk about modules, ask about a lesson that did not work, and find the people who wrote what you use. It is being built. Until it opens, this host carries the development copy of the docs.

## What is here now

- **Docs in development** under [/docs](/docs): the pages of docs.entrosana.app as a working copy. Changes land here first, get read, and are taken over to the published docs from the console.
- **Nothing else yet.** No accounts, no posts. The forum arrives when it is ready.

## What the forum will be

- Threads per module, so a question about a lesson lands with the people who wrote it.
- A place for translations and rewrites of the docs to be read aloud and argued over before they go live.
- The same accounts as the Hub: one sign-in, the same names, the same record trail.

## Until then

Questions go to [info@entrosana.com](mailto:info@entrosana.com) or through the [request form](https://entrosana.app/request). Reports about a module go through the form on the module's page.
