# Accounts

One account per person, tied to an e-mail address, without a password. This page explains standings and roles, what your account page holds, how sign-in and devices work, and how an account ends.

## Standings

| Standing | Who | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| collector | everyone who registers | a workspace, a profile, invitations, points, reports, a list of services |
| contributor | granted by the team after an application | proposing modules under your name, editing your published modules |
| reviewer | the team | reading proposals and applications, admitting, holding or rejecting them |
| owner | the person running the Hub | publishing and taking modules back, granting roles, and everything above, plus the machine's own services in the services list |

Roles are granted only on the server, by the owner; there is no button for it. To become a contributor, open your account page and apply with a short text. The team decides, and you get a mail with the reason.

## Your account page

Under [entrosana.app/me](https://entrosana.app/me) you find:

- **Team**, for reviewers and the owner: the queue and the waiting list, with a link to the review console.
- **Profile**: name, organisation or school, role, a line about you, a longer biography, place, a contact address for your public page, website, languages, picture.
- **Your modules**: every published module you contribute to, with the editor.
- **Journal**: short entries, each one public or private as you choose.
- **Invite**: send someone an invitation by e-mail.
- **Devices**: every signed-in session with its date; end one or all.
- **Record trail and visibility**: what the chain records about you and what is public.
- **Export and erase**.

## Your public page

Every account with a name has a public page at `/p/<number>`, also reachable under `/contributors/<name>`. It shows name, standing, picture, organisation, role, the line about you, biography, place, the contact address if you set one, website, languages, the date you joined, published modules and public journal entries. The sign-in address is never shown.

## Sign-in and devices

- The sign-in link is good for 20 minutes. Opening it signs that device in for 30 days.
- Sign-in requests are limited to five an hour per address and per origin, and the answer never says whether an address is known.
- When a device signs in to an existing account, the address gets a mail with the time and a rough origin.
- Erasing the account and changing a module's attribution need a link younger than 15 minutes.

## Applications and invitations

- **Apply** for contributor standing with a text. It is kept with your account and deleted 24 months after the decision.
- **Invite** by e-mail address, with a short message. The link is good for 7 days and creates a collector account when it is opened. Unanswered invitations are removed after 180 days.

## How an account ends

- **Erase** on your account page: address, profile, applications, journal, invitations and services go. For each published module you choose whether it keeps your name.
- **Dormant**: no sign-in for 24 months brings a notice by mail. Three months later without a sign-in the account is erased the same way.
- The owner account cannot erase itself; ownership is handed over first.

## Age

Accounts are for people of 13 and older. Younger learners take part through a teacher or a parent, who holds the account. The Hub stores no age; it only asks at registration.
