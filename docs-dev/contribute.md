# Contribute

Modules are published under the name of the person who wrote them, under the AGPL-3.0, after a review by the entrosana team. This page describes the way from an idea to a module in the catalogue, and what you can do with a module once it is yours.

## The rules, short

- Everything in the Hub is AGPL-3.0. What you send in is published under it, under your name.
- No commercial or religious intent, no personal data of children, nothing you do not hold the rights to.
- Disagree with the work, not with the person.
- Schools: the Hub holds no pupil accounts and no pupil data. Teachers register as adults, and a module report names the group, never the child.

The full text is one page: [guidelines](https://entrosana.app/guidelines).

## Propose a module

1. Open [the proposal form](https://entrosana.app/contribute/submit).
2. Fill in your name, your e-mail address, a title, the area (social, nature-based, technical or other), a summary, and the files or links that belong to it.
3. If you are not signed in, a confirmation link goes to your address. The proposal counts once you open it.
4. You get a status page for the proposal. The team reads it, may ask by mail, and decides: not yet, or no, always with the reason. Publishing is the owner's step alone; no module enters the catalogue without it. Admit and publish is one step for the owner, so a published module appears in the catalogue at once.

Every step is a line in the record trail.

## Once a module is published

- **It is yours to edit.** Under «Your modules» on your account page, or with the **Edit** button on the module's card, you change title, lede, text, facts and links. Every save stamps the last update.
- **State.** A module is «only an idea», «still in development» or «finished». The state shows on the card and on the module page.
- **More than one contributor.** A module can have several contributors, and each of them can edit it. A contributor is added by the e-mail address of an approved account.
- **Points and reports.** Anyone signed in can give a module one point, and take it back. Anyone can report a module through the form on its page; the report goes to sygnif@entrosana.com and the team answers.

## Invitations

From your account page you can invite someone by e-mail address, with a short message. The link is good for seven days and creates a collector account when it is opened.
