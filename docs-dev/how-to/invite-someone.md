# Invite someone

An invitation is a link that creates a collector account for the address you invite.

## Steps

1. Set your name first; the invitation carries it.
2. On [your account page](https://entrosana.app/me), block **Invite**, enter the e-mail address and, if you like, a message of up to 500 characters.
3. Press **Send**. The person gets a mail with a link that is good for seven days.
4. When they open it, a collector account exists and their device is signed in. Your account page shows the invitation as accepted.

## Rules

- You cannot invite your own address or an address that already has an account.
- Unanswered invitations are removed after 180 days.
- The invited person can erase their account like anyone else; your invitation then shows the address as erased.
