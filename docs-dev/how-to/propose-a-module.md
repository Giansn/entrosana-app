# Propose a module

From an idea to a proposal the team can read. No account is needed to send one; publishing needs contributor standing.

## Before you start

- A title of 3 to 160 characters and a summary of up to 4000.
- The area: social, nature-based, technical, or other.
- The files or pages the module consists of, as links. Files are linked, not uploaded here; if you keep them in your Hub account, [Keep files](/how-to/keep-files) gives you a public link per file.
- Nothing personal about children in any of it.

## Steps

1. Open [the proposal form](https://entrosana.app/contribute/submit).
2. Fill in your name and e-mail address, the title, the area, the summary, and the links. You may attach the record trail of the project you worked in; it is verified before publication.
3. Press **Send**.
4. If you are not signed in, a confirmation link goes to your address. Open it within an hour; the proposal counts once you do. Signed in, it counts at once.
5. You get a link to the status page of the proposal. Every decision appears there with its reason; you also get each by mail.

## After sending

- **Withdraw** it on the status page while it is open.
- **Choose the attribution:** whether the module keeps your name if you ever erase your account.
- **Apply as contributor** on your account page if you are not one yet; the gate before publishing requires it.

> **Warning:** Everything you send in is published under the AGPL-3.0, under your name. Send only what you hold the rights to.
