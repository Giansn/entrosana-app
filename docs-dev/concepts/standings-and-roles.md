# Standings and roles

One account per person. What an account may do is its standing, and the standing comes from roles that only the owner grants.

## The four standings

| Standing | Who | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| collector | everyone who registers | workspace, profile, journal, files, invitations, points, reports, services |
| contributor | granted after an application | everything above, plus editing the published modules you contribute to and being added to a module |
| reviewer | the team | reading proposals and applications, admitting, holding or rejecting them |
| owner | the person running the Hub | publishing and taking modules back, granting roles, everything above, plus the machine's own services in the services list |

## How standing changes

- **Collector to contributor.** Apply on your account page with a text of 20 to 2000 characters. The team decides and you get a mail with the reason. An approved application writes the contributor role; a declined one writes nothing. Decided applications are deleted 24 months later.
- **Reviewer and owner.** Granted only on the server by the owner, with a command line. There is no button for it, and no application.
- **The console.** The owner's console acts through a service account that holds the owner role, so a decision made there is the owner's decision.

## What the team cannot do

A reviewer cannot publish, take a module back, grant roles, or erase anyone's data. Publishing is the owner's step alone, see [Review and publishing](/concepts/review-and-publishing).

> **Note:** Every grant and every decision is a line in the [record trail](/concepts/record-trail) with the account number of the person who made it.
