# Getting started

The entrosana Hub is the working side of entrosana: where modules for schools are proposed, reviewed, published and kept alive, and where you keep an eye on the things that must run for your work. This page takes you from nothing to a signed-in account with a profile.

## What the Hub is

- **Tools and Modules** are the catalogue: what entrosana publishes, sorted into Nature, Social and Technical. Every module has a page with its state, its contributors, a way to give it a point and a form to report a problem.
- **Contribute** is where a module comes in: the proposal form, the rules, and a status page for every proposal with each decision and its reason.
- **Workspace and Projects** are your own board: lanes, cards and due dates, kept in your browser. A first project walks you through contributing a module.
- **Running services** is your list of things that must keep running, checked by the Hub. See [Services](/services).
- **Record trail** is the Hub's memory: every decision is a line in a chain that anyone can verify.

## An account

You register with an e-mail address. There is no password.

1. Open [entrosana.app/me](https://entrosana.app/me) and enter your address. Tick the box that says you are 13 or older, or that you register on behalf of a younger learner as a teacher or parent.
2. The Hub mails you a link. Open it on the device you want to use. The link is good for 20 minutes and signs that device in for 30 days.
3. Every account starts as a **collector**: a workspace, invitations, points and reports. To publish under your name you apply for **contributor** standing on your account page. The team decides and you get a mail with the reason.

When another device signs in to your account, your address gets a mail with the time and a rough origin. Under «Devices» on your account page you can end any session, singly or all at once.

## Your profile

A name is needed before you propose or contribute, because work is published under it. Everything else is optional: organisation or school, role, a line about you, a website, languages, a picture. Every account with a name has a public page. It shows your name, standing, picture, published modules and the journal entries you mark public. Your e-mail address is never public.

## Your data

- **Export** everything the Hub holds about you as JSON, from your account page.
- **Erase** your account from the same page. A fresh sign-in link is asked for first, and you choose whether published modules keep your name.
- An account with no sign-in for two years gets a notice and is erased three months later, the same way.

The details are in the [privacy statement](https://entrosana.com/privacy/) and the [guidelines](https://entrosana.app/guidelines).
