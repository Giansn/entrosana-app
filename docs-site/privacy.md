# Privacy

The binding text is the [privacy statement](https://entrosana.com/privacy/) on entrosana.com, in English and German. This page is the short version for people who use the Hub: what is stored where, for how long, who else sees it, and what you can do yourself.

## Where your data rests

Your data rests on entrosana's own server in Switzerland, with one encrypted backup copy at Cloudflare R2. Processing is a separate matter: delivery runs through Cloudflare (USA), the code lives at GitHub (USA), and mail to sygnif@entrosana.com is read by Claude, a language model run by Anthropic (USA). Where the pages say «Swiss/EU», they mean where stored data rests, not the seat of every provider that processes it.

## What the Hub stores about you

| What | Stored | For how long |
|---|---|---|
| Your account | e-mail address, time of registration, a hashed token per signed-in device, what you add to the profile | until you erase it; unconfirmed addresses go after 7 days |
| Proposals | name, title, area, summary, files or links, the record trail you attach | an unconfirmed proposal goes after 7 days; a published module stays while it is in the catalogue |
| Applications | the text you write to become a contributor | 24 months after the decision |
| Requests and reports | name, address, organisation, kind, text | 180 days |
| Invitations | the address you invite and your message | unanswered ones go after 180 days |
| Services | name, kind, the address to check, results | until you remove them |
| The record chain | account numbers and hashes, no names or texts | permanently; it proves the history was not rewritten |
| Request log | IP address, path, status, duration, account number when signed in | 14 days |

What stays in your browser: your projects, the record trail, your theme and your sign-in token. None of it is sent unless you submit something.

## What is public

Every account with a name has a public page: name, standing, picture, organisation or school, role, the line about you, the longer biography, place, a contact address you set yourself, website, languages, the date you joined, published modules and the journal entries you mark public. Never your sign-in address. Published modules carry your name under the AGPL-3.0, and the catalogue file lives in the code repository at GitHub.

## Mail

The mailboxes are at Proton (Switzerland). Confirmation, decision and notification mails come from the Hub through Proton. Mail to sygnif@entrosana.com, reports included, is read by entrosana's own software and passed to Claude (Anthropic, USA) to sort it and draft replies; people decide what goes out. Mail to info@entrosana.com is read by people.

## Sign-in and devices

A sign-in link is good for 20 minutes and signs a device in for 30 days. When another device signs in, your address gets a mail with the time and a rough origin, and you can end any session under «Devices». Erasing your account or changing a module's attribution needs a link younger than 15 minutes.

## What you can do yourself

- Export everything the Hub holds about you as JSON.
- Change or empty every profile field, remove the picture, change or delete journal entries.
- Erase your account. You choose whether published modules keep your name.
- Withdraw an undecided proposal on its status page.
- An account with no sign-in for 24 months gets a notice and is erased three months later.

For anything else write to info@entrosana.com; the answer comes within 30 days. You may complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner in Bern or, in the EU, to your supervisory authority. No automated decisions are made about you.

## Children and schools

The Hub holds no pupil accounts and no pupil data. Teachers and schools register as adults, and a module report names the group, never the child. The processing register is available on request at info@entrosana.com.

## Who else receives data

Cloudflare, Inc., USA (delivery, security, tunnel, one encrypted backup copy); Proton AG, Switzerland (mail); GitHub, Inc., USA (the code repository, which includes the catalogue with published contributors' names); Anthropic, PBC, USA (the language model that reads the sygnif@ mailbox). Nothing is sold and nothing is shared for advertising. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies of our own.
