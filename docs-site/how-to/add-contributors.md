# Add contributors to a module

A module can have several contributors. Each of them can edit it and appears on its card and page.

## Requirements

- You are a contributor of the module yourself, or you are the owner.
- The person you add has an account with contributor standing and a name. A collector cannot be added; ask them to apply first.

## Steps

1. Open the module's editor, see [Edit a published module](/how-to/edit-a-module).
2. Under **Contributors**, enter the e-mail address the person registered with and press **Add**. The address is looked up, never shown; the card shows the name.
3. To remove someone, press **Remove** next to the name. A module keeps at least one contributor.

## What the team can do

The owner's console lists every module with its contributors and can add or remove them the same way, for example when a module changes hands.

> **Note:** Adding and removing are lines in the record trail. The person added gets no mail; tell them.
