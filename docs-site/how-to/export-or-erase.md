# Export or erase your data

Both are on [your account page](https://entrosana.app/me), block **Export and erase**.

## Export

**Download everything** gives you a JSON file with your account, profile, journal, applications, proposals with their files and records, requests under your address, invitations, services, files list, and the lines of the record trail that concern you.

## Erase

1. Press **Erase my account**.
2. The Hub asks for a fresh sign-in: open a new link from your mailbox, then press again. This protects against a forgotten open session.
3. For each published module you contributed, choose whether it keeps your name.
4. Confirm. Address, profile, applications, journal, invitations, services and files go. Modules you chose to keep stay under your name; the others leave the catalogue.

The lines in the record trail stay with your former account number, which then points at nothing.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no sign-in for 24 months gets a notice by mail. Without a sign-in in the three months after it, the account is erased the same way, with the attribution you set on each module.

> **Owner:** The owner account cannot erase itself; ownership is handed over on the server first.
