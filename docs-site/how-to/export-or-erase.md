# Export or erase your data

Both start on [your account page](https://entrosana.app/me): export under **Your data**, erasure in the **Danger zone** at the bottom.

## Export

**Download my data** gives you a JSON file with your account, profile, journal, applications, proposals with their files and records, requests under your address, invitations, services, files list, and the lines of the record trail that concern you.

## Erase

1. Press **Erase my data** in the Danger zone. It opens your [contribution status page](https://entrosana.app/contribute/status), where the erasure happens.
2. For each published module you contributed, choose whether it stays published under your name.
3. Press **Erase my data** and confirm. If you signed in more than 15 minutes ago, the Hub first asks for a fresh sign-in: press **Send me a fresh link**, open it on the same device, then press again. This protects against a forgotten open session.
4. Address, profile, applications, journal, invitations, services and files go. Modules you chose to keep stay under your name; the others leave the catalogue.

The lines in the record trail stay with your former account number, which then points at nothing.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no sign-in for 24 months gets a notice by mail. Without a sign-in in the three months after it, the account is erased the same way, with the attribution you set on each module.

> **Owner:** The owner account cannot erase itself; ownership is handed over on the server first.
