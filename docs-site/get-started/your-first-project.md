# Your first project

The Workspace is your own board: projects with lanes and cards, kept in your browser. Nothing on it is sent to the Hub.

## What you find on a new board

Three projects are proposed to every board once, the Hub's own current work:

- **Docs in plain language, in several languages**, 8 steps.
- **Translate the website text**, 8 steps.
- **Work out the requests**, 6 steps.

Each step is a card with a title, a note and instructions. Open a card to read the instructions, tick it when done, give it a due date, or move it between lanes. The rail counts done and open cards per project.

## Start from a checklist

Every tool and module in the catalogue carries a checklist. On its page, **Open the checklist** creates a project from it on your board. The same checklists are offered under **+** next to Projects in the rail.

## Start from nothing

**+** in the rail also offers an empty project. Give it a name, add cards, sort them by hand or by due date.

## Where the board lives

In your browser's local storage, under the Hub's origin. Clearing site data removes it, and another browser does not see it. Export it under **Export trail** if you want to keep a copy; the export carries the board and the record trail.

> **Tip:** The record trail in the rail, All records and Today, lists what you did on the board, with a hash per entry. **Verify chain** checks that nothing was altered.
